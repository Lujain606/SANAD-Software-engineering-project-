SANAD
Students Supporting Students
Project Definition
SANAD is a peer-support platform that connects university students who
need academic assistance with students who can provide help in a specific
subject or skill.
Objectives
Connect: Connect students who need academic help with students
who can provide it.
Support: Make it easier for students to request and provide academic
help.
Organize: Allow students to arrange suitable sessions for academic
support.
Scope
The project focuses on peer-to-peer academic support among university
students. It covers requesting and offering help in academic subjects and
technical skills, arranging support sessions, and providing basic feedback.
The platform does not include paid tutoring, professional instructors, or
completing academic work on behalf of students
